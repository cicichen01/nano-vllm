KEY FACT: W_UK is PER-HEAD (that's what makes MLA differ from MQA).
The shared latent c is d_c=512, but EACH head decompresses it with its
OWN slice W_UK^(h) (128x512). If W_UK were shared -> that would be MQA.

  per head       = 2,097,152
  x 32 heads = 67,108,864   (all heads)
